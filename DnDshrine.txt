On the 25th of October the DnD group that I've been part of for 7 (!!!) years had our final main campaign session. 
<b>The Slumber Party</b> began its adventures back in 2019 when all of us were still university students. There have been lots of 
life changes since! It started with a sticker on our DM's laptop and soon we had found enough people to kick off our DnD sessions.
<br><br>
For a while, we were able to meet up IRL for our sessions, but when it was time for some of us to move to other cities, we continued on Discord, Roll20 and D&D Beyond. Throughout the years, we've had shorter and longer breaks between each session - D&D players will recognize the challenge of finding a suitable schedule for every party member. 
We started as a group of 6 and later continued until "the end" as a group of 5. 
<br><br>
Our DM made the call (a good call, I think everyone in the party would agree) to wrap up our main campaign with one final remote session, and that any future D&D that we play together would be IRL. 
We've had lots of fun during our remote sessions, but there's a special kind of magic to playing D&D together IRL. :-) For now, we all still live a train ride away from each other, 
which makes IRL sessions possible. We've had a couple of those in the summertime, playing hilarious oneshots and "multi-shots", and with some guest players, as well. 
<br><br>
Finishing the main Slumber Party campaign (with no permanent character deaths! incredible) pushed me to finally start digitalizing my D&D notes.
