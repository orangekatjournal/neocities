artist alley

2026/09/19

When I as younger, at an age when I hadn't started working yet, I used to look forward to one day commissioning artists. I've done that a few times by now, and I've also gone to a bunch of artist alleys. I love it!

Even more so nowadays with shitty AI-generated visual content having infested every crevice of the internet. I'm glad and grateful that there are still artists out there that have the energy to keep creating and that they keep trying to sell their work. I love supporting them and I get to enjoy a nice little dopamine rush from looking at their beautiful work.
