youtube confessional

2026/11/14

One of my favorite things about YouTube is seeing people using the comment section as a confessional, but without the religious guilt. Strangers share stories of celebrations and grievances, both heartwarming and heartwrenching. Maybe they're looking for words of comfort from other strangers who can relate, or maybe writing down the words and letting them float out into the unknown without any expectations is enough to lighten their hearts.

It's such a human thing to do, and one of the human traits that I find positive. I thought it would be fun to document memorable comments that I stumble upon, in the following categories: grief, hope, nostalgia, and kindness in the comment section of food videos.

Clicking on a comments will lead you to the video it's from, so these will double as glimpses into my Liked Videos. :-)
